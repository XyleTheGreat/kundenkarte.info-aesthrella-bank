NOTES FROM DEVELOPERS

KUNDENKARTE BANKING GUIDELINES

Currency Conversion
The banking system of Kundenkarte utilizes the Aesthrellan Taresh (AET) currency, establishing a standard conversion rate where 1 AET is equivalent to ₱0.25.

Account Security
Card identification numbers are uniquely structured with distinct Prefix, PIN, and CRN segments, and these credentials must be kept strictly confidential to prevent unauthorized access.

Expiration Policy
Expired cards are permanently purged from the database immediately upon system check, making it vital to initiate a renewal 5 to 10 days before your card expires to avoid service disruption.

Transaction Requirements
Providing a screen capture of the interface is a mandatory security requirement for processing all withdrawal transactions.

Responsible Usage
Users are strongly urged to manage their accounts responsibly and utilize the Kundenkarte platform wisely to maintain optimal financial security and system efficiency.

Prohibited Activities
Cardholders are strictly prohibited from utilizing the Kundenkarte Loyalty Black Card for unauthorized gambling activities, illicit betting pools, fraudulent transactions, or any form of credential sharing, as any violation will trigger an immediate asset freeze and permanent termination of membership.
